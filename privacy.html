Privacy policy - Katharina

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Katharina is a private assistant application used only by its owner and household. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, Katharina accesses the owner's Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Drive to read, organize and draft content for the owner.

How data is used

Data is processed on the owner's private computer and sent to Anthropic's Claude service solely to generate responses for the owner. Data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than assisting the owner.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
